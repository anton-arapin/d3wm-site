About

I'm a second-year PhD student at the University of Michigan, advised by Bernadette Bucher in the Mapping and Motion Lab. My research sits at the intersection of geometric generative models, computer vision, and robotics. Specifically, I am interested in World Models that preserve geometric structure as scenes evolve, generative models that learn and traverse the action-conditioned data manifold, and their applications in Robotics.

Before Michigan, I was a software engineer at Google, working on a large-scale machine learning system for modeling and predicting user behavior, with a focus on the accuracy, quality, and efficiency of training and inference. Previously, I also collaborated with Junchen Jiang at the University of Chicago on neural video codecs, and with Kostas Daniilidis at the GRASP Lab on robot exploration and navigation.
